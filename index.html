About The Project

Why Microplastics?

Microplastics are very small plastic particles that can enter water systems through plastic waste, industrial processes, wastewater and environmental pollution.

This project demonstrates how artificial intelligence and image analysis can assist in detecting and analyzing possible microplastic particles in drinking water samples.
